# Opportunities and Companies

SkillSpan is not a conventional job board and does not guarantee employment. Its purpose is to build measurable career readiness, verified practical experience, and evidence-based professional visibility.

Learners may receive explainable project and opportunity recommendations subject to eligibility, current skills, growth value, career relevance, interests, availability, and platform rules.

Authorized companies may use consent-aware Talent Discovery to search permitted candidates using job-relevant information such as target role, verified skills, readiness, project experience, language, location, and availability.

Candidate information remains subject to privacy, visibility, and consent rules. Talent Discovery supports human review and must not be presented as an automated hiring decision.

The approved SRS does not promise employment, automatic placement, or a general service for contracting complete external teams.
