# What Makes SkillSpan Different?

SkillSpan connects career readiness, skill evidence, career direction, practical experience, evaluation, recommendations, and professional visibility in one traceable journey.

## Key Differences

- Evidence-based readiness: role-specific readiness and skill results are supported by approved evidence and explanations rather than self-declaration alone.
- Explainable career intelligence: readiness, skill gaps, roadmap actions, and recommendations include human-readable reasons and limitations.
- Practical experience: learners can participate in approved simulated or company-sponsored projects with controlled requirements, milestones, submissions, and evaluation.
- Living Digital Professional Record: verified projects, evaluations, skills, achievements, and contribution evidence can update an evidence-based professional record.
- Growth-oriented recommendations: project matching balances current capability with learning value, career relevance, interests, and availability while enforcing eligibility first.
- Consent-aware Talent Discovery: authorized companies may discover permitted candidates using job-relevant evidence without unrestricted access to private learner data.

SkillSpan is not merely a course platform, job board, freelancing marketplace, portfolio site, or generic AI career-advice tool.

The approved SRS does not guarantee continuous personal mentoring for every learner and does not require a post-project interview as the universal verification method.
