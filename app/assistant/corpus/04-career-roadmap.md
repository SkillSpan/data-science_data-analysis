# Career Roadmap

The SkillSpan Career Roadmap is a personalized, ordered development plan based on the learners approved target role, verified skill gaps, prerequisites, priority, practical value, and available time.

Roadmap actions may include assessments, approved learning resources, practice tasks, simulated projects, and real projects. Actions must respect prerequisites and include objectives and completion criteria.

The roadmap may organize work into Foundations, Core Skills, Applied Practice, and Career Readiness phases. Duration is estimated from required effort and weekly availability.

When approved evidence, assessments, project evaluations, availability, or the career goal materially changes, the roadmap may be recalculated as a new version without overwriting historical completed work.

SkillSpan also identifies an explainable Next Best Action based on priority gaps, prerequisites, the current phase, and practical value.

The approved SRS does not guarantee that every learner is continuously assigned a personal mentor.
