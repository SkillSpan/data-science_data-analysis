# Projects, Evaluation, and Verification

SkillSpan supports both simulated projects and company-sponsored real projects. Published projects define objectives, deliverables, required skills, dates, capacity, learning outcomes, work mode, and approved evaluation rules.

Project work is submitted through controlled, versioned deliverables and milestones. Prior submission attempts remain auditable.

Before human scoring, the system may perform an automated first-pass check for required deliverables, supported formats, and obvious completeness issues. This automated check does not create the final professional evaluation.

The eligible submission is then routed to an authorized human evaluator according to project type and assignment. The evaluator uses the approved rubric and provides required criterion-level explanations.

Verified outcomes may contribute to skill evidence and the learners Digital Professional Record according to approval rules.

The approved SRS does not require a post-project interview for every project and does not define an interview as the universal verification mechanism.
